About Me

I am a first-year postgraduate student in Biomedical Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati, specializing in Regenerative Medicine, Stem Cells, and Therapeutics. My academic journey is defined by a commitment to interdisciplinary research, integrating computational methodologies with rigorous wet-lab experimentation.

My previous research spans computational biophysics, including extensive molecular dynamics simulations at IIT Mandi, molecular parasitology at IBAB Bengaluru, and infectious disease biology at NHRI Taiwan. This diverse foundational training has equipped me to understand complex biological systems at both the molecular and organismal levels. I am currently directing my expertise towards the fields of tissue engineering and 3D bioprinting. By combining my background in computational modeling and molecular biology, I aim to contribute to the development of next-generation regenerative therapies and engineered biomaterials.

Selected Projects

Plasmodium falciparum Multi-Omics Portal

Developed a web platform integrating data from PlasmoDB, UniProt, STRING, NCBI, and AlphaFold DB. The portal provides interactive visualisations of the proteome, genome, transcriptome, interactome, spatial localisation, and gene ontology of the parasite.

View Portal

Automated Molecular Docking Pipeline

Developed a reproducible Python-based molecular docking workflow integrating AutoDock Vina, MDAnalysis, and ProLIF. This tool allows for automated protein-ligand interaction analysis and structural visualization.

Phytocompound Repurposing for Gastric Cancer

Screened phytocompounds against gastric cancer receptors using SwissADME. Performed molecular docking for binding-affinity ranking and executed molecular dynamics simulations of the highest-ranked ligands to compute RMSD, RMSF, SASA, and radius of gyration.
